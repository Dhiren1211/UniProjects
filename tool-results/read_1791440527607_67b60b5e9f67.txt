     1→     1→# UniProject Design System
     2→     2→
     3→     3→> **Visual reference:** [Flokka](https://flokka.me/)\
     4→     4→> **Purpose:** Visual design specification for UniProject --- colors,
     5→     5→> typography, layout, components, states, responsive behavior, and
     6→     6→> interaction style.
     7→     7→
     8→     8→## 1. Design Direction
     9→     9→
    10→    10→UniProject should borrow the **visual feeling** of Flokka rather than
    11→    11→copy its branding.
    12→    12→
    13→    13→Flokka's current landing page uses a focused student-network message,
    14→    14→short sections, generous whitespace, strong typography, simple
    15→    15→navigation, and clear primary actions. Its content hierarchy moves from
    16→    16→a large statement → supporting explanation → feature/value blocks →
    17→    17→process → final CTA. citeturn1view0
    18→    18→
    19→    19→### UniProject visual personality
    20→    20→
    21→    21→-   **Modern academic**
    22→    22→-   **Friendly and student-first**
    23→    23→-   **Clean rather than corporate**
    24→    24→-   **Content-led**
    25→    25→-   **High whitespace**
    26→    26→-   **Strong typography**
    27→    27→-   **Soft surfaces and rounded components**
    28→    28→-   **One recognizable accent color**
    29→    29→-   **Minimal decorative noise**
    30→    30→
    31→    31→The interface should feel like a combination of:
    32→    32→
    33→    33→`University archive + student portfolio + community network`
    34→    34→
    35→    35→not a traditional university administration portal.
    36→    36→
    37→    37→------------------------------------------------------------------------
    38→    38→
    39→    39→## 2. Brand Principles
    40→    40→
    41→    41→### 2.1 Content is the hero
    42→    42→
    43→    43→Projects, students, screenshots, technologies, and documentation are the
    44→    44→primary visual content.
    45→    45→
    46→    46→Do not let UI decoration compete with project content.
    47→    47→
    48→    48→### 2.2 One strong accent
    49→    49→
    50→    50→Use one primary brand color consistently for:
    51→    51→
    52→    52→-   Primary buttons
    53→    53→-   Active navigation
    54→    54→-   Links
    55→    55→-   Selected filters
    56→    56→-   Important badges
    57→    57→-   Focus states
    58→    58→-   Small visual accents
    59→    59→
    60→    60→Avoid using many saturated colors at once.
    61→    61→
    62→    62→### 2.3 Soft neutral canvas
    63→    63→
    64→    64→The main page background should be a very light warm/cool neutral rather
    65→    65→than pure white.
    66→    66→
    67→    67→Cards can remain white so that content feels elevated without heavy
    68→    68→shadows.
    69→    69→
    70→    70→### 2.4 Typography creates hierarchy
    71→    71→
    72→    72→Large headings should carry most of the visual identity. Body text
    73→    73→should remain quiet and highly readable.
    74→    74→
    75→    75→### 2.5 Rounded, but not playful
    76→    76→
    77→    77→Use medium-to-large corner radii for cards and controls.
    78→    78→
    79→    79→Avoid excessive "bubble UI".
    80→    80→
    81→    81→------------------------------------------------------------------------

    83→    83→# 3. Color System
    84→    84→
    85→    85→## 3.1 Recommended UniProject palette
    86→    86→

    88→    88→the clean, restrained visual direction of Flokka rather than copied
    89→    89→brand colors.

    92→    92→  ------------------ ----------- ------------------------------
    93→    93→  `primary-500`      `#5B5CE2`   Main brand/accent

    95→    95→  `primary-700`      `#3F40B8`   Active/pressed
    96→    96→  `primary-100`      `#E9E9FF`   Soft accent surface
    97→    97→  `primary-50`       `#F5F5FF`   Very light accent background
    98→    98→  `canvas`           `#F7F7F5`   Global page background

   100→   100→  `surface-muted`    `#F1F1EF`   Secondary surfaces
   101→   101→  `text-primary`     `#17171C`   Headings/body emphasis
   102→   102→  `text-secondary`   `#5F6068`   Supporting text
   103→   103→  `text-tertiary`    `#8A8B93`   Metadata/captions
   104→   104→  `border`           `#E5E5E2`   Dividers/input borders
   105→   105→  `border-strong`    `#D2D2CE`   Hover/active borders
   106→   106→  `success`          `#16835B`   Success/verified
   107→   107→  `success-soft`     `#E7F6EF`   Success background
   108→   108→  `warning`          `#B7791F`   Warning

   112→   112→  `info`             `#3978B8`   Informational
   113→   113→  `info-soft`        `#EAF3FC`   Informational background
   114→   114→
   115→   115→### 3.2 Color ratio
   116→   116→
   117→   117→Use approximately:
   118→   118→
   119→   119→-   **70%** neutral canvas/surfaces
   120→   120→-   **25%** text/borders/secondary neutrals
   121→   121→-   **5%** primary accent and semantic colors
   122→   122→
   123→   123→The accent should feel special. Do not turn the entire interface purple.
   124→   124→
   125→   125→### 3.3 Dark mode
   126→   126→
   127→   127→Dark mode is optional for v1.
   128→   128→
   129→   129→If implemented:
   130→   130→
   131→   131→``` text
   132→   132→background      #111116
   133→   133→surface         #19191F
   134→   134→surface-muted   #222229
   135→   135→text-primary    #F7F7F8
   136→   136→text-secondary  #B5B5BD
   137→   137→text-tertiary   #85858E
   138→   138→border          #303039
   139→   139→primary         #7778F0
   140→   140→```

   144→   144→# 4. Typography
   145→   145→
   146→   146→## 4.1 Font
   147→   147→
   148→   148→### Primary
   149→   149→
   150→   150→**Pretendard**
   151→   151→
   152→   152→Recommended for UniProject because the product is expected to contain
   153→   153→Korean and English university content.
   154→   154→
   155→   155→``` css
   156→   156→font-family:
   157→   157→  "Pretendard",
   158→   158→  -apple-system,
   159→   159→  BlinkMacSystemFont,
   160→   160→  "Segoe UI",
   161→   161→  sans-serif;
   162→   162→```
   163→   163→
   164→   164→### Alternative
   165→   165→
   166→   166→If Pretendard is unavailable:
   167→   167→
   168→   168→``` text
   169→   169→Inter → Apple SD Gothic Neo → Segoe UI → sans-serif
   170→   170→```
   171→   171→
   172→   172→## 4.2 Font weights
   173→   173→
   174→   174→Use only four main weights:
   175→   175→
   176→   176→  Weight   Purpose
   177→   177→  -------- -------------------------
   178→   178→  400      Body
   179→   179→  500      Labels/buttons
   180→   180→  600      Card titles/subheadings
   181→   181→  700      Major headings
   182→   182→
   183→   183→Avoid using 800/900 except for a deliberate marketing hero.
   184→   184→
   185→   185→## 4.3 Type scale
   186→   186→
   187→   187→  Token          Size   Line height   Weight
   188→   188→  ------------ ------ ------------- --------
   189→   189→  Display        56px          1.05      700
   190→   190→  H1             44px           1.1      700
   191→   191→  H2             32px           1.2      700
   192→   192→  H3             24px          1.25      700
   193→   193→  H4             20px           1.3      600
   194→   194→  Body Large     18px           1.6      400
   195→   195→  Body           16px          1.55      400
   196→   196→  Body Small     14px           1.5      400
   197→   197→  Label          13px           1.4      500
   198→   198→  Caption        12px           1.4      400
   199→   199→
   200→   200→### Heading rules
   201→   201→
   202→   202→-   Keep headings short.
   203→   203→-   Prefer sentence case.
   204→   204→-   Avoid all-caps headings.
   205→   205→-   Use `letter-spacing: -0.02em` for large headings.
   206→   206→-   Do not use heavy shadows or gradients on text.
   207→   207→

   210→   210→# 5. Spacing System
   211→   211→
   212→   212→Use a 4px base unit.
   213→   213→
   214→   214→``` text
   215→   215→4   = 1xs
   216→   216→8   = 2xs
   217→   217→12  = xs
   218→   218→16  = sm
   219→   219→20  = md-sm
   220→   220→24  = md
   221→   221→32  = lg
   222→   222→40  = xl
   223→   223→48  = 2xl
   224→   224→64  = 3xl
   225→   225→80  = 4xl
   226→   226→96  = 5xl
   227→   227→128 = 6xl
   228→   228→```
   229→   229→
   230→   230→### Common usage
   231→   231→
   232→   232→-   Icon-to-label: `8px`
   233→   233→-   Input padding: `12–16px`
   234→   234→-   Card internal padding: `20–24px`
   235→   235→-   Section gap: `48–80px`
   236→   236→-   Major page sections: `80–120px`
   237→   237→-   Mobile page padding: `20px`
   238→   238→-   Desktop page padding: `32px`

  1220→  1220→# 33. Do / Don't
  1221→  1221→
  1222→  1222→## Do
  1223→  1223→
  1224→  1224→-   Use large whitespace.
  1225→  1225→-   Use strong typography.
  1226→  1226→-   Keep cards clean.
  1227→  1227→-   Use one primary accent.
  1228→  1228→-   Show project screenshots prominently.
  1229→  1229→-   Make creator identities visible.
  1230→  1230→-   Keep metadata compact.
  1231→  1231→-   Use subtle borders and shadows.
  1232→  1232→-   Make filtering easy.
  1233→  1233→-   Maintain consistent spacing.
  1234→  1234→
  1235→  1235→## Don't
  1236→  1236→
  1237→  1237→-   Do not copy Flokka's exact logo, branding, illustrations, or
  1238→  1238→    proprietary visual assets.
  1239→  1239→-   Do not use a rainbow palette.
  1240→  1240→-   Do not turn every card into a colorful gradient.
  1241→  1241→-   Do not use excessive shadows.
  1242→  1242→-   Do not put huge amounts of metadata on cards.
  1243→  1243→-   Do not make every page look like a social-media feed.
  1244→  1244→-   Do not use all-caps text everywhere.
  1245→  1245→-   Do not rely on hover as the only way to reveal information.
  1246→  1246→-   Do not sacrifice readability for visual effects.
